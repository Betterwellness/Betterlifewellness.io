Cholibrium Review: Ingredients, Benefits, Price, and Safety

Cholibrium is a dietary supplement from Simple Promise that features a blend of 10 mushroom ingredients. It is marketed to adults interested in supporting cardiovascular wellness, antioxidant support, and healthy cholesterol levels already within the normal range. The formula includes Reishi, Lion's Mane, Cordyceps, Shiitake, Maitake, Turkey Tail, Chaga, Royal Sun Agaricus, White Button Mushroom, and Wood Ear Fungus.

According to the product label, each serving consists of two capsules, and each bottle contains 60 capsules for a 30-day supply. The sales page recommends taking two capsules daily with water, preferably 20–30 minutes before a meal.

Before purchasing, review the complete Supplement Facts label, current package prices, and refund conditions.
